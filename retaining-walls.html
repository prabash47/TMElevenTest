Don't Risk Costly Retaining Wall Failures

Retaining walls are not simply a landscaping feature. Poor drainage, unsuitable materials, incorrect construction methods and inadequate consideration of ground pressure can lead to expensive problems later.

Built With Stability in Mind

TM Eleven Melbourne approaches retaining wall projects with a focus on strength, stability and proper preparation. We consider the surrounding landscape, soil conditions, drainage requirements and the intended purpose of the wall before construction.

Common problems can occur when drainage is neglected, soil type and ground conditions are ignored, materials are mismatched or structural shortcuts are taken. Compliance and local requirements also need to be considered when planning retaining wall work.

Our goal is to provide retaining wall solutions that work with your landscape while helping protect your property from unnecessary structural and drainage problems.
