ABOUT TM11

Melbourne's Trusted Outdoor Construction Team

At TM11, we believe quality work starts with understanding exactly what our customers need. We take the time to understand your ideas, property and goals before delivering a solution that is practical, durable and built to last.

With more than 10 years of experience in Melbourne, our team provides professional concreting, landscaping, fencing and outdoor construction services across Melbourne and surrounding areas.

From a new driveway or concrete entertaining area to fencing, turf, landscaping, decking and pergolas, we manage each project with attention to detail and a strong commitment to quality workmanship.

We are an Australian registered company, licensed and insured, with a focus on reliable service, honest communication and quality results. Our goal is simple — to deliver outdoor spaces that look great, perform well and add long-term value to your property.
