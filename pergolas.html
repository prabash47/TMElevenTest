Extend Your Outdoor Living Space

A pergola can transform an ordinary outdoor area into a comfortable and inviting place to entertain, relax and enjoy your home. TM Eleven Melbourne provides pergola solutions designed to complement the existing style of your property.

From modern freestanding structures to pergolas connected with decks and entertaining areas, we focus on creating practical outdoor spaces that provide shade, shelter and a strong visual connection between your home and garden.

Your pergola can also be planned as part of a larger outdoor project, combining decking, landscaping, paving and entertaining areas into one cohesive design.
